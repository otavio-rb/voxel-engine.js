  • voxelScene() — volume de voxels gerado por ChunkData, malhado por ChunkGeometry e iluminado pelo Sky. Serve a colisão, raycast, partículas, entidades e passes de tela.voxelScene() — a voxel volume generated by ChunkData, meshed by ChunkGeometry, and lit by Sky. Serves collision, raycasting, particles, entities, and screen passes.
  • worldSandbox() — ProceduralWorld com chunk workers, só onde o streaming é o assunto: mundo, LOD, dimensões, fluidos, jogador e interação.worldSandbox() — ProceduralWorld with chunk workers, only where streaming is the point: world, LOD, dimensions, fluids, player, and interaction.
  • engineSandbox() — a engine inteira num worker, com OffscreenCanvas, para VoxelEngine, runEngineWorker, EngineContext e comandos.engineSandbox() — the whole engine in a worker, with OffscreenCanvas, for VoxelEngine, runEngineWorker, EngineContext, and commands.
